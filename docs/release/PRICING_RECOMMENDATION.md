## 1. Executive Summary & Recommendation for David

### 🎯 Recommended Launch Price: **NGN 1,800** (Nigeria) / **$1.99 USD** (Global Base)

#### Why NGN 1,800?
1. **Psychological Sweet Spot in Nigeria**:
   - In Nigeria, NGN 1,800 is comparable to the price of a cinema soda, a shawarma snack, or two weeks of data subscription.
   - For an avid anime or movie fan who gets **420 handcrafted levels** (over 40 hours of gameplay) with zero annoying ads, NGN 1,800 feels like an immediate, guilt-free high-value purchase.
   - At NGN 1,000, some buyers wonder if it's too cheap or low quality. At NGN 3,500+, conversion rates drop significantly for indie debut titles. NGN 1,800 signals premium polish while remaining highly accessible.

2. **Global Purchasing Power Parity (PPP)**:
   - Setting the base international price to **$1.99 USD** (or £1.49 / €1.79) positions CineCross against top indie word games like *Knotwords*, *Puzzmo*, and *Letterpress*.
   - In the US and Europe, $1.99 is the default impulse-buy tier for mobile puzzle games.

## 3. Revenue Breakdown (What David Receives)

Google Play takes a developer service fee:
- **Google Play 15% Tier**: Google charges only **15%** on the first $1,000,000 USD of earnings each year for enrolled developers (standard registration).
- **Your Net Share**: **85%** of every sale!

### Example Per 1,000 Sales:
- **Nigeria (1,000 sales @ NGN 1,800)**:

## 4. Launch Promotion & Sale Strategy

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Critical Google Play Rule for the Owner**:
> - **Google does NOT allow a free app to be converted into a paid app later.**
> - **However, a PAID app can run temporary promotional discounts or free sales!**
> - Starting paid upfront protects your business model permanently.

### Proposed Promotional Calendar:
1. **Week 1–2 (Launch Blitz)**:
   - Launch at the regular price of **NGN 1,800 ($1.99)**.
   - Run word-of-mouth campaigns in Nigerian university communities, tech circles, anime fan groups, and movie forums.
2. **Month 2 (First Weekend Flash Sale)**:
   - Use Google Play Console's built-in **"App Sale"** feature.
   - Discount the game for 48 hours to **NGN 1,000 ($0.99 USD)** (45% OFF).
   - This triggers Google Play's strikethrough price tag (`~~$1.99~~ $0.99`), which surges store conversion.
3. **Holiday Special (December Anime & Cinema Season)**:
   - Run a 5-day holiday discount: **NGN 1,200 ($1.29 USD)** with a seasonal banner.
