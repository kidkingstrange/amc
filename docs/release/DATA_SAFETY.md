# Google Play Console Data Safety Form Guide

> **Audience**: David (for copy-pasting directly into Play Console > App Content > Data Safety)  
> **Status**: Verified 100% Offline / Zero Collection

---

## Question-by-Question Form Responses

### Section 1: Data Collection and Security
1. **Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?**
   - **Answer**: `No`
2. **Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?**
   - *(Disabled automatically when answering No above)*
3. **Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?**
   - **Answer**: `No` (or select: *"My app does not collect user data so no data deletion mechanism is required"*).

---

### Section 2: Data Types (All Categories)
Because the initial answer is **No**, all individual data categories below are marked as **NOT COLLECTED**:
- **Location**: Not collected
- **Personal info** (Name, email, phone, etc.): Not collected
- **Financial info** (Credit card, bank account, purchase history): Not collected *(Purchases are handled directly by Google Play)*
- **Health and fitness**: Not collected
- **Messages**: Not collected
- **Photos and videos**: Not collected
- **Audio files**: Not collected
- **Files and docs**: Not collected
- **Calendar**: Not collected
- **Contacts**: Not collected
- **App activity** (Page views, clicks, interactions): Not collected
- **Web browsing**: Not collected
- **App info and performance** (Crash logs, diagnostics): Not collected
- **Device or other IDs**: Not collected

---

### Section 3: Summary Display in Google Play Store
Once submitted, Google Play will display the following badge on your public store page:

```
🛡️ No data shared with third parties
The developer says this app doesn't share user data with other companies or organizations.

🛡️ No data collected
The developer says this app doesn't collect user data.

🔒 Security practices
Data isn't encrypted in transit because no data is transferred.
You can request that data be deleted by clearing the app's local storage or uninstalling.
```

This badge provides massive trust for paying players!
