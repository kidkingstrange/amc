## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **CineCross: Anime & Movies** ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. CineCross is designed as a premium, privacy-first, fully offline game. 

This Privacy Policy explains how our App handles user information. The short answer: **We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information whatsoever.**

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
Unlike many modern mobile games, CineCross operates 100% locally on your Android device:
- **No Personal Identification Information**: We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, email address, phone number, address, or age.
- **No Account or Authentication**: CineCross requires no registration, sign-in, or social media login.
- **No Device Identifiers**: We do not collect your Android Advertising ID (AAID), IMEI, MAC address, IP address, or hardware identifiers.
- **No Location Data**: We do not request access to GPS, Wi-Fi location, or network location.
- **No Analytics or Telemetry**: We do not include Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Mixpanel, or any tracking SDKs.
- **No Advertising SDKs**: The app contains zero ads and zero third-party ad networks (no AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, etc.).
- **No Network Permissions**: The app does not request or use Android network permissions (`android.permission.INTERNET`). It cannot transmit data even if it wanted to.

---

## 3. Data Stored Locally On Your Device
All game progress (including completed levels, puzzle star ratings, unlocked Trivia Dex cards, daily challenge streaks, and sound/theme preferences) is saved solely within your device's private local application storage using standard Android sandboxing.

- This data never leaves your device.
- We cannot access, view, or restore your progress if you uninstall the app or clear your app data.
- You can permanently delete all local game data at any time by uninstalling the application or selecting "Clear Storage" in your Android system settings.

## 4. Children’s Privacy
CineCross is suitable for audiences of all ages, including children and families. Because we collect zero personal data from any user, we strictly comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play’s Families Policy.
